Results intelligence

Results management

Capture, review, verify and collate party-agent result submissions.
SDP agent submissions, internally verified party records and officially declared INEC results will remain distinct. Party submissions will never be labelled as official results.
▥Results workspace awaiting configuration

Result-sheet evidence, review history, corrections, versioning and collation will be implemented with strict audit controls.
