Results intelligence

Results management

Capture, review, verify and collate party-agent result submissions.
SDP agent submissions, internally verified party records and officially declared INEC results remain separate data classes. A party submission is never an official result.
RESULTS PROCESS & STATUS DEFINITIONS
1SubmissionAgent report and result-sheet evidence received
2Evidence reviewSource, location and image quality checked
3VerificationArithmetic and authorised review completed
4CollationVerified records grouped by electoral level
5Official declarationINEC declaration recorded separately with source
MANDATORY RESULT CLASSIFICATIONS
AGENT SUBMISSION · UNVERIFIEDINTERNALLY VERIFIED PARTY RECORDOFFICIAL INEC DECLARATION
The system will retain the original submission, evidence reference, reviewer identity, review decision, correction history and timestamps. Totals that fail reconciliation checks will be flagged for review, not silently changed.
▥Results workspace awaiting backend connection

No results are displayed in this preview. Live submission, verification, reconciliation and collation will be enabled after secure database and access-control implementation.
