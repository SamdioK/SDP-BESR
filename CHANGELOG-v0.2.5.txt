SDP-BESR Interface v0.2.5

Enhancements inspired by publicly documented US election results-reporting and election administration practices:
- Dashboard Election Integrity & Data Assurance panel covering evidence/source traceability, result reconciliation, duplicate/correction controls, and audit/chain-of-custody history.
- Results Management process flow distinguishing agent submission, evidence review, internal verification, collation, and official INEC declaration.
- Explicit labels for unverified agent submissions, internally verified party records, and official INEC declarations.
- Clear notices that these controls are interface/planned workflow descriptions only; they are not active until implemented and enforced by the Supabase backend.

No live election results, operational figures, or news are fabricated in this preview.
Existing official SDP logo, election countdowns, map, and election dashboards are retained.
